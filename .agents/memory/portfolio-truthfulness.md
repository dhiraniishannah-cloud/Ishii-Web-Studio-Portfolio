This portfolio presents website concepts and demos, not paid client work. Do not claim a client count, affiliation, or commission.

Project intake is not ready for real client submissions until a file-capable endpoint is configured and verified. Never show success without an actual successful response from that service, and never claim uploaded files are securely stored without verifying the provider's protections.

**Why:** The user explicitly distinguishes portfolio demos from client work and prohibits fake submission or unsupported privacy/security claims.

**How to apply:** Keep portfolio copy clear about concept status. Keep intake status transparent until a real endpoint is connected and tested.
